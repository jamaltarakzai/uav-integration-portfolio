Case C-001 · flight log analysis

Multirotor upset at 854 s

In steady forward flight the aircraft suddenly spun and rolled inverted. The controller drove yaw and throttle to their limits for 8 s before it recovered. Battery, GPS, radio link, flight mode and the attitude sensor were all normal: the evidence points to a sudden, temporary loss of thrust on one motor, propeller or ESC.

Ruled out

  • Battery: healthy before the event; sag followed throttle and recovered
  • GPS: 28 satellites, 1 m accuracy
  • Radio link and failsafe: RC rate steady 66-67 Hz, mode never changed
  • Sensor or controller fault: INS steady 200 Hz, error word 0

Evidence for motor / prop / ESC

  • Target heading held at 352° while the aircraft spun
  • Yaw output at its -20% limit and throttle at 84% together
  • Yaw output sat at -5 to -7% through normal flight: an existing imbalance
  • Recovery after 8 s: loss was temporary (e.g. ESC desync, loose prop)
